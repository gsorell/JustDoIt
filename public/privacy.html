Privacy Policy

Cadence · Last updated June 18, 2026

Cadence ("the app") is a personal commitment and habit tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: Cadence does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Cadence has no accounts, no login, no servers that store your information, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

What the app stores, and where

Your commitments, check-ins, streaks, charms, and settings are saved locally on your own device (in the operating system's app storage on mobile, or your browser's local storage on the web). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Notifications

Cadence schedules check-in reminders as local notifications on your device. It does not use a remote push service, and no notification data is sent to or through any server.

Sharing

If you tap a "Share" option, the app hands a short message to your device's own share sheet, and you choose where it goes. We do not see, receive, or store anything you share.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you are fully in control: delete a commitment within the app, or uninstall the app, to remove the associated data. We hold nothing on our end to delete.

Children's privacy

Cadence does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
